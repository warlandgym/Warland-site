Abdul Razak Alhassan is a former UFC fighter and lifelong martial artist with nearly three decades of experience in combat sports. Originally from Ghana, his journey has taken him from humble beginnings to competing on one of the biggest stages in the world: the Ultimate Fighting Championship.

As the founder of War Land Gym, Razak brings elite-level experience in MMA, boxing, kickboxing, judo, and strength & conditioning to athletes of all levels. His coaching philosophy is built on discipline, consistency, and real-world results, helping beginners build confidence while pushing serious athletes to reach their full potential.

Razak created War Land to be a place where kids grow into leaders, athletes sharpen their edge, and everyday people build strength, both physically and mentally. With a passion for mentorship and community, he is committed to helping every member step onto the mat and rise like a warrior.
